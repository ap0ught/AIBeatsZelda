# Phase 9: passive is losing

**Three corrections from the person watching.** First: in the Zol room, the bot stood still for
sixteen seconds because a Zol sat in its lane. "You don't have to kill the slimes, but you have
to make progress." Second: it sat in a doorway for fifteen seconds refusing to step into a blade
trap's row. "You can walk right through, they won't hit you. Be aggressive." Third, the standing
rule: an unplanned hit is a mistake, but standing still is not safety, it is slower and it gets
you hit anyway.

**What changed.** When the next step is blocked by an enemy, the bot now looks for a detour that
keeps clear of everything nearby and takes it. If there is no detour, the blocker is "in the
way" and gets killed. Waiting is the last resort and it is capped. The Zol room went from 989
frames to 558, clean. Trap lines are crossed freely and never lingered in; the bot only reacts
to a trap that is actually sliding. And it always steps out of a doorway before doing anything,
because the game disables attacks there.

**The raft.** Milestone 3's verified run skipped something: Level 3 also holds the raft, and
Level 4 sits on an island that needs it. So the run has to detour north: clear the ring room
for its key, unlock north, get through a room whose shutter turns out to open not by killing but
by pushing a block, pass an old man whose speech freezes Link for a moment, and reach a room of
eight Keese where another block hides the stairs to a basement with the raft. Two facts learned
the hard way in that wing: shutters in this game have more than one trigger, and blocks will not
move until the room's enemies are dead. The map site's convention of drawing Link where a
staircase is turned out to be the clue.

**Reading the cartridge.** The Level 3 header in the ROM names its entrance, its Triforce room
and its boss room, and the bot's decoded door table has matched every door it has opened since.
