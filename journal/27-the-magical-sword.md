# 27 — The Magical Sword

**Where we are:** back inside Level 9, standing in the same room as before — but carrying the
strongest sword in the game and thirteen heart containers instead of eleven.

## The trip
Out of Level 9 without a scratch, then around Hyrule on the recorder's whirlwind:

1. **Warp to Level 1**, three screens to a lone tree, burn it: "TAKE ANY ONE YOU WANT" — a heart
   container. **Twelve.**
2. **Warp to Level 2**, around to the giant rock, bomb its corner: another. **Thirteen.**
3. **Warp to Level 6**, three screens to the graveyard's corner, push the middle gravestone up.
   The stairs appear under it, and the old man hands over **the Magical Sword**.
4. **Warp to Level 5**, walk the mountain road back to Spectacle Rock, and in through the door the AI
   blew open weeks ago — still open.

## What made it work
Nothing in this trip was run on faith. Every new move — the burned tree, the bombed rock, the
gravestone, the whirlwind route — was first tried hundreds of times from saved copies of the game,
and each one that "worked in a test" was tested again inside the real search before the real run
used it. That second test is what caught Link standing three pixels off the tree, and the
whirlwind counting its own rides as failures.

The full run, replayed from power-on in a fresh emulator, matches frame for frame: **329,529
frames**, about an hour and a half of game time.
