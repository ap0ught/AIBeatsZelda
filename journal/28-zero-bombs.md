# 28 — Zero bombs

**Where we are:** back inside Level 9 with the Magical Sword and thirteen hearts — and, it turns out,
not a single bomb. Level 9 is built out of bombable walls.

## The pile behind the wall
The nearest bombs were a pile in room 0x25 that only appears once the room is cleared.

- **The sword** couldn't clear it: two Like Likes, two Zols and two Bubbles (which can't be killed,
  and switch the sword off if they touch Link). Fifty attempts, fifty timeouts.
- **Arrows** cleared it every time. The bombs appeared... and Link couldn't reach them.

The room is split into three lanes by two solid columns of blocks, and the bombs appear in the
middle lane — whose only ways in are two walls you open *with a bomb*. The AI tried everything that
doesn't need one:

- **The boomerang**, which fetches items: thrown from three heights, it never got past the blocks.
- **Pushing** each of the seven blocks: none of them moves.

The walkthroughs reach that pile by bombing down from the room above. Which takes a bomb.

## Meanwhile, two emulators knocked each other over
While all this was going on, the AI tried to record the video of the Magical Sword run at the same
time as the run itself. The recording died halfway, and the run's search emulator crashed five times
in the same nine minutes. Windows recorded no crash for either. Recorded again on its own, the video
finished in ten minutes. New house rule: never record while a run is going.

## Going shopping
So: leave the dungeon and buy bombs. One whirlwind note facing *left* moves the counter from Level 5
back to Level 4 — whose door is an island next to a shop that sells four bombs for 20 rupees. Raft to
shore, walk round the lake, buy twice, one note facing *right* back to Level 5, walk the mountain
road, and in again.

The human added three rules on the way, now part of the AI's memory: there are bomb-capacity upgrades
(100 rupees each, in Levels 5 and 7), always pick up bombs that drop, and don't spend them unless it's
really worth it. The fighting code now walks out of its way for any bomb on the floor.
