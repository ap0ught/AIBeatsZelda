# Level 3: Manji (GameFAQs guide by TheRewster, v1.11) — saved 2026-09-12

Source: https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/nes/563433-the-legend-of-zelda/faqs/75987/level-3-manji
(ROM room ids in brackets.)

- Entry [7C] -> west [7B]: Zols, Key #1 (on the floor). Up [6B]: Zols must die for Key #2.
- Up [5B]: three Red Darknuts. Hit from side/back; they rarely get knocked back; strike and
  retreat; lie in wait near a block and hit them as they walk past, before they can turn. All three
  dead -> 4 Bombs.
- Bomb the east wall [5C]: ignore the Darknuts, go north [4C]: blade traps, Map, Zols optional.
  (East wall of [4C] is a bombable shortcut to the boss.) Unlock west [4B]: kill three Zols ->
  Key #3. Unlock north [3B]: two Zols + traps; move the WESTERN block to open the north door.
  [2B] old man ("Did you get the sword from the old man on top of the waterfall?"). West [2A]:
  Key #4 guarded by Keese.
- Back to two rooms south of the old man [4B]; unlock west [4A]: narrow corridor of Keese; kill
  them all (arrows/boomerang for the far ones) -> 4 Bombs appear in the corridor. West [49]: Zols,
  Keese, two Bubbles (invincible; touching one disables the sword for a few seconds). Key #5.
- South [59]: Darknuts; unlock east [5A]: traps + Keese, Compass. Back west [59]: defeat the
  Darknuts to open the SOUTH door. [69]: EIGHT Red Darknuts. You do NOT have to defeat them (4
  Bombs if you do): clear a path to the STAIRS in the EASTERN part of the room. Basement: RAFT.
- Back: from the raft room north [59] then east [5A], east twice, defeat the three Darknuts to
  open the east door; then Zols and Keese (avoid Bubbles) to open the north door (+5 rupees);
  north: Manhandla.
- Manhandla: four heads, unblockable fireballs; gets faster per head lost. Bombs: one blast can
  take several heads, or all four. Heart container, then north for the Triforce.

Speed-relevant facts: only one of the keys is truly needed on the fast route (the TAS gets by
with the floor keys); the eight-Darknut room is a dash to the east stairs, not a fight.
