# Level 4: Snake (GameFAQs guide by TheRewster, v1.11, 2018) — saved 2026-09-12

Source: https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/nes/563433-the-legend-of-zelda/faqs/75987/level-4-snake

Reaching it: the Level 4 dock is at map square F-6 (the dungeon island is F-5, overworld room
0x45). Step onto the dock and the raft carries Link north to the entrance. (The other dock, P-4,
leads north to an old man's cave with a heart container / 2nd potion.)

Route (ROM room ids in brackets, decoded 2026-09-12):
- Entry room [71]. West door -> Keese room [70]: kill all Keese -> Key #1 appears.
- Back to the entry, north [61]: three Vires (each splits into two red Keese when killed; no
  drops from Vires with the wooden sword). Kill all three, unlock the EAST door [62] (dark room,
  four Vires, Compass on the far side). Optional for speed.
- From [61] north [51]: Keese; Key #2. Head west [50], dark, Vires, go north [40]: Zols, Key #3.
  North door -> [30].
- [30]: dark, Vires and Bubbles; north door blocked by a one-space water chasm (needs the ladder).
  Use a key to go EAST [31]: Vires on narrow walkways; killing all Vires opens the east door.
- [32]: two Bubbles, two Zols, two Like Likes (never let one swallow Link: it eats the magic
  shield). Kill everything except Bubbles -> secret passage (block push) -> cellar [60]: STEPLADDER.
- Back west to [30]; with the ladder cross the chasm north [20]: Vires (stand over water to be safe
  from walkers). East [21]: dark, Gels, the Map; bomb north -> [11] (10 rupees, all four walls
  bombable; west [10] is Manhandla, skip). North [01]: Key #4 guarded by Keese (ladder). East twice
  -> [02] -> [03?]: dark room with blade traps, go south -> [12]: five Vires, move the WEST block to
  open the east door -> Gleeok [13].
- Gleeok: two heads, each breathes unblockable fireballs. Run in, stab a head, retreat. A destroyed
  head detaches and flies around invulnerable, still shooting. Spread hits between heads. Wooden
  sword: 4 hits per head (Mariner) / 10 then 6 (Red Candle). Heart container appears; north to
  the Triforce [03].

Speed notes (Mariner): "Head up, ignore the Vires, and up again. Grab the key and hang a left. The
room of diagonal blocks goes dark; avoid the Vires, move up. Kill the Zols to make the key appear,
grab it, move up. Take a right and kill all the Vires. Move right again and go down the secret
passage for the ladder. Head left two, then up. Move right into a dark room of Gels, hug the wall
to the top and bomb up. Bomb right. Kill the Vires, move the block, head right. Gleeok: 4 hits per
head." Keys used in Level 4: 4. Dark rooms: navigate from memory, don't waste time on the candle.
