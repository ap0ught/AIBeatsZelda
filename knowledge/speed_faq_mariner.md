# The Legend of Zelda — Speed FAQ/Walkthrough v1.0 by Mariner (GameFAQs, 2005) — saved 2026-09-12

Source: https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/nes/563433-the-legend-of-zelda/faqs/35129
Non-Up+A, no money-making game, no first-enemy bomb luck. ~45-49 minutes real time.
Item order it uses: sword, L1 (bow, bombs from octorok/goriya), heart container (seashore),
L2 (Dodongo always drops bombs if stunned+swordered), white sword, blue candle, arrows, bait,
L3 (raft), L4 (ladder), L5 (recorder), L7, magical sword (graveyard), L6 (Gohma: one arrow),
L8, L9 (silver arrows), Ganon.

## Overworld to Level 1
Start: sword, then up, right, up 3, left. Kill the blue Octorok right of the L1 entrance (may drop
bombs, ~30 s saved). Kill only Octoroks in the way.

## Level 1
Right: kill the Stalfos with the key, grab it, leave. (No bombs: left, kill all Keese for another
key.) Back to the entrance, up, up: kill all Stalfos for the key (or bomb up). No bombs: left, up,
right ignoring enemies. Up, key, up again: kill the Goriyas fast for a key, left for the BOW.
Return to the map room, right (optional: Goriyas -> boomerang; Goriyas tend to drop bombs), right,
key, up. Ignore Wallmasters. Aquamentus: 6 hits. Keys 0, ~4 min.

## Level 1 -> Level 2
Right, down 2, right, down, right 2, down: seashore. Bomb the wall left: heart container. Up 2,
right 2, up, left, up: Level 2.

## Level 2
Right 1 then straight up, taking three keys: right after going right, the Rope room (4 rooms up),
the Moldorm room. Room before Dodongo is Goriyas that must die: one bomb each; bombs reappear
after. Dodongo: bomb in the mouth, then a second bomb in front while he swallows -> stunned ->
sword. Always drops bombs this way. Keys 3, ~9 min.

## Level 2 -> Level 3 (white sword, candle, bait, arrows)
Down, right, up: touch the rightmost Armos -> 30 rupees. Up. (Optional letter / 100 rupees.) Left
one, bomb the right side of the giant rock: heart container. Up, left 2, up: WHITE SWORD (beam the
blue Lynel or run past). Down, follow the river left, down the eastern shore of the lake, all the
way south to one square above the bottom, then left: room above the start. Bomb the wall: 30
rupees. Left: buy the blue CANDLE. Left again, cross the bridge. With 140 rupees: up 2, buy ARROWS,
up, buy BAIT, back down 3. Left 2: vertical wall of trees; burn the topmost single tree: 100
rupees. Back right, down, right: Level 3.

## Level 3
Left: kill the Zols (slow, rupees + hearts), grab the key, up. Up again, left. Left again through
the locked door to the room of RED DARKNUTS: kill them all to open the door (as fast as possible).
Move DOWN to the room with the RAFT: move immediately right and down into the passageway as fast
as possible and you shouldn't get hit (may have to smack one Darknut and run through him while he
flashes). Back up and right two to the three-Darknut room. Bomb the wall (kill the Darknuts if
you need bombs; at least 4 needed now). Through the bombed wall, up, bomb right again.
Manhandla: drop a bomb so he's on top of it when it explodes; kills him outright or takes off
arms; one arm left -> two sword hits. Keys 4, ~15 min.

## Level 3 -> Level 4
Left, up, right to exit the dead forest. (Arrows/bait shops straight up if not done.) Right,
cross the bridge, up, sail from the dock to Level 4.

## Level 4
Vires: they fly back and split into two Keese on an obstacle; hit them near an obstacle and slice
both Keese with one swing. Dark rooms: don't use the candle, memorize them. Up, ignore the Vires,
up again. Key, left. Diagonal-block room goes dark: avoid Vires, move up. Kill the Zols for the
key ([-] shaped water), up. Right: kill all Vires. Right again: down the secret passage for the
LADDER. Left two (avoid Vires), up. Right: dark room, Gels, hug the wall to the top and bomb up.
Bomb right. Kill the Vires, move the block, right. Gleeok (2 heads): run up and swing; 4 hits per
head. Keys 4, ~19 min.

## Level 4 -> Level 5
Sail down, right, back up. Right: burn the fifth tree from the right: piece of heart. Right.
Up 2, right into the desert, right to the giant-rock screen, up and left into the Lost Hills,
keep going up: Level 5.

## Level 5
Up, bomb left, bomb left again: blue Darknuts (drop hearts). Passage, left, more Darknuts:
RECORDER. Back to the room above the start (bomb the Gibdo first). Kill the Gibdos, key, up and
right. Up again, kill the Gibdos: key. Up twice, key, left two. Left: Digdogger: recorder up
close, then sword, 4 hits. Keys 4, ~26 min.

## Level 5 -> Level 7
Face up, blow the recorder twice -> Level 3. Left, up 2, left, up. Blow the recorder: Level 7.

## Level 7
No keys needed; ~5 bombs. Up (Goriyas may drop bombs), bomb up. Up: kill Keese and Goriyas to open
the door. Ignore Digdogger, left, up. Use BAIT, up. Dark room: move up to the centre before going
right to cross water. Locked door, bomb right. Ignore the passage (red candle), bomb right again.
Right: Digdogger (use boomerang after the recorder). Up, bomb right: avoid Bubbles, kill 5
Wallmasters (arrows), move the block, passage. Bomb right. Aquamentus. Keys 1, ~31 min.

## Level 7 -> Level 6 (magical sword)
Down, left, down: Lost Woods: up, left, down, left. Up, right, up 2: graveyard top right; push the
grave: MAGICAL SWORD. Down, right, up: Level 6.

## Level 6
Left (Wizzrobes). Up (Zols optional). Up: kill the Keese to open the door, key. Up (traps won't
hurt going straight up). Kill everything (yellow Wizzrobes first, then blue), push the block, up.
Bomb right, key, down. Kill the Vire, move the block, right. Kill everyone: secret passage. Out:
Zols for hearts, down. Kill the Vire: key, left, switch to arrows, up. Gohma: eye is open on
entry: step right, shoot up. Keys 2, ~36 min.

## Level 6 -> Level 8
Recorder (face up, once from L3 warp / twice from L4 warp) -> Level 2. Down, right, down twice.
Burn the tree in the middle of the path, enter Level 8.

## Level 8
Up (ignore Manhandla), bomb up. Kill all blue Darknuts avoiding statue blasts, key, up twice. More
Darknuts, then right. Need 7+ bombs after this: kill everyone here for bombs if low. Down the
passage. Pols Voice with arrows, bomb up (ignore the key). Gleeok (4 heads): stay close, swing.
Keys 2, ~41 min.

## Level 8 -> Level 9
Recorder once (face up) -> Level 1. Right, up, left. Up, cross the river, up. Left two: Spectacle
Rock, bomb the left one: Level 9.

## Level 9
Up, up. Kill Zols and Like Likes: key, bomb left. Lanmolas (arrows if not full health). Passage,
right past Like Likes (stun/flash to walk through). Dark room, 3 blue Darknuts: straight right.
Need 4 bombs (7 with the red ring): kill the Patra here if short. Up through the locked door, bomb
left (old man's hint). Kill Wizzrobes, push the block, passage. Left twice. Kill the Patra,
passage (ignore the key). Bomb up, kill all, passage: SILVER ARROWS. Back down, kill all, passage
back. From the Patra+key room, up. Ignore Like Likes, up, up to the gray-sand room, bomb left.
Kill Wizzrobes, push a block, passage. Bomb left, kill Zols and Like Likes, passage: Patra room,
kill it, up. Ganon: invisible, 4 sword hits turn him brown (15 with wooden), then silver arrow.
Figure-eight movement; fireballs hurt less than contact. ~49 min.
